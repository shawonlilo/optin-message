Opt-In Message
Consent-first messaging — Simple App. Your data stays on this device.
SIMPLE APP — no setup, no server, sending is simulated

Messaging people actually said yes to.

Most small businesses get their WhatsApp number banned because they message people who never agreed. Opt-In Message flips that: people subscribe, confirm, and can stop anytime — and every step is proven and saved. This app runs entirely on your device: no account, no server, nothing running in the background.

1 · People opt in

A person fills the opt-in form and ticks the consent box. Nothing is sent yet — they start as Pending.

2 · They confirm (double opt-in)

They reply YES to a confirmation. Only then are they Subscribed, with a saved consent proof: time, method and the exact consent text.

3 · A human approves every campaign

Messages are risk-checked first, and sending stays locked until a person approves. That gate is enforced, not a suggestion.

4 · STOP always wins

Anyone can stop at any time. A stopped contact is permanent — they can never be messaged or re-added.

Try it

Open Contacts & opt-in, add a contact, confirm them, then write a campaign in Campaigns, approve it and send (simulated). Every step lands in the Audit log. The app starts with 3 clearly labelled demo contacts from the fictional Dhaka Threads shop so it is never empty.

Write a campaign

Write a message. A plain rule-based check scores its spam/ban risk as you type. Saving creates a campaign that is waiting for approval — it cannot be sent until a human approves it. “Sending” here is simulated: it writes a delivery log entry per Subscribed contact, and Pending and Stopped contacts are always skipped. No real message ever leaves this app.
